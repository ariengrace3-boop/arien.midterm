About Me

Background

My name is Arien Grace Alforque Monin, and I am 19 years old.
I was born on October 29, 2006, at Orlanes Clinic in Naga City.
I have brown eyes and dark brown hair. My blood type is O.

I consider myself a friendly and jolly person who enjoys spending time with others and creating a positive atmosphere.
I like making people smile and building good relationships with the people I meet.
I am also independent and try my best to handle responsibilities and grow as a person.

I enjoy learning new things, whether in school, life skills, or experiences that help me improve myself.
Learning is an important part of becoming a better and stronger person in the future.

My favorite colors are blue and black because they look calm, simple, and elegant.

Education

I completed my elementary education at Naga Central Elementary School.
I studied junior high school at Naga National High School.
I completed senior high school at Exceed Learning Center.

Currently, I am pursuing my college education at Cebu Institute of Technology – University (CIT-U), where I continue developing my skills in Information Technology.

Hobbies

My hobbies include dancing, drawing, and painting. Dancing lets me express emotions and energy, while drawing and painting allow me to explore creativity. These activities relax me and help me express myself beyond words.
